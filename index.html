📐 Chapter 8: Formulas & Points to Remember

1. Range & Coefficient of Range

\(R = L - S, \quad \text{Coefficient of Range} = \dfrac{L - S}{L + S}\)

2. Standard Deviation (\(\sigma\)) & Variance (\(\sigma^2\))

• Direct Method: \(\sigma = \sqrt{\dfrac{\sum x_i^2}{n} - \left(\dfrac{\sum x_i}{n}\right)^2}\)

• Mean Method: \(\sigma = \sqrt{\dfrac{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2}{n}}\)

• Assumed Mean Method: \(\sigma = \sqrt{\dfrac{\sum d_i^2}{n} - \left(\dfrac{\sum d_i}{n}\right)^2}\)

• Step Deviation (Grouped): \(\sigma = c \times \sqrt{\dfrac{\sum f_i d_i^2}{N} - \left(\dfrac{\sum f_i d_i}{N}\right)^2}\)

• First \(n\) natural numbers: \(\bar{x} = \dfrac{n+1}{2}, \quad \sigma = \sqrt{\dfrac{n^2 - 1}{12}}\)

3. Coefficient of Variation (C.V.)

\(\text{C.V.} = \dfrac{\sigma}{\bar{x}} \times 100\%\)

4. Probability & Addition Theorem

• \(P(E) = \dfrac{n(E)}{n(S)}, \quad 0 \le P(E) \le 1, \quad P(\bar{E}) = 1 - P(E)\)

• \(P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)\)

• \(P(A \cup B \cup C) = P(A)+P(B)+P(C) - P(A \cap B) - P(B \cap C) - P(A \cap C) + P(A \cap B \cap C)\)
